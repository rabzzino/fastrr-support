## Common questions

### How do I delete my account?

Open Fastrr → **Profile → the gear icon → Account details → Delete Account**. This permanently removes your account and all its data. It doesn't cancel an App Store subscription: cancel that in your Apple ID settings (below).

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Open your device **Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Fastrr → Cancel**.

### Does my Apple Health data leave the device?

Your activity, heart rate and sleep data from Apple Health stay on your device. Weigh-ins you bring in from Health become weigh-ins in Fastrr and sync with your account like the ones you type. Nothing from Health is sent to analytics providers or any other third party.

### How do I export my data?

Open Fastrr → **Profile → the gear icon → Data → Export my data**. It makes a CSV file of your fasts, weigh-ins and water that a spreadsheet can open.
