## Common questions

### How do I delete my account?

Open Fastrr → **Profile → Delete Account**. This permanently removes your account and all your data.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Open your device **Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Fastrr → Cancel**.

### Does my Apple Health data leave the device?

No. HealthKit data is used only on-device to power your insights. It is never sent to our servers or any third party.

### How do I export my data?

Open Fastrr → **Profile → Export Data** to generate a PDF summary or CSV of your fasting history.
